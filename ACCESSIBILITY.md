# Accessibility Statement

## Commitment to Digital Accessibility

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually strive to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 Level AA).

## Accessibility Measures

This website incorporates the following design and technical practices:
- **Semantic HTML**: Proper use of landmarks (`main`, `nav`, `section`, `footer`) and hierarchical headings (`h1` through `h6`).
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Interactive elements (buttons, links, color picker, drawers) are fully focusable and operable via keyboard.
- **Color Contrast**: Text and visual elements meet or exceed contrast ratios across dark mode and theme accent modes.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` browser settings to minimize animations for motion-sensitive users.
- **Alternative Text**: Descriptive `alt` attributes on meaningful images and iconography.

## Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need content in an alternative format, please contact us at **admin@knownaspratyush.online**. We review all accessibility feedback promptly.
