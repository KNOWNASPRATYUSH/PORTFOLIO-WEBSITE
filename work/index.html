CASE STUDY 01 • COFFEE & CRAVINGS

Coffee & Cravings — Real-Time QR Dining & KDS Engine

The Challenge & Problem

Artisan cafes experience severe bottlenecking during peak rush hours. Paper menus created order latency, manual cashier queues slowed throughput, third-party delivery aggregators levied steep 20–30% commissions, and table-ordering lacked tamper-proofing against unauthorized order injection.

Architecture & Engineering Approach

Engineered a zero-dependency native Node.js core backend eliminating npm supply-chain vulnerabilities. Implemented cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signed table tokens across 6 tables to prevent order spoofing, Upstash Redis REST KV storage for sub-10ms in-memory menu state, an instant audio chime Barista Kitchen Display System (KDS), dynamic UPI payments with Razorpay webhook reconciliation, and compliance under India's DPDP Act 2023.

Measurable Results & Impact

71 of 71 automated tests passing (100% test coverage); sub-second live order dispatch directly to barista screens; 100% elimination of aggregator fees; zero third-party cookie leakage.

CASE STUDY 02 • INDIA FITNESS

India Fitness — High-Velocity Digital Athletics Platform

The Challenge & Problem

With over 1,500 active athletes across three gym branches in Patna (Sipara, Kurthaul, Sampatchak), the business was hampered by an outdated, sluggish template website with high Cumulative Layout Shifts (CLS), slow mobile loading, and confusing workout discovery.

Architecture & Engineering Approach

Engineered a modern Server-Side Rendered (SSR) web app using Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Implemented hardware-accelerated GSAP animations locked at 60FPS, structured PostgreSQL relational database schema for branch facilities and trainer schedules, and built interactive client-side tools including a BMI calculator and dynamic routine planner.

Measurable Results & Impact

Fluid 60FPS route transitions with zero layout shift (CLS = 0); instantaneous mobile rendering; measurable surge in online membership registrations across all three Patna locations.
