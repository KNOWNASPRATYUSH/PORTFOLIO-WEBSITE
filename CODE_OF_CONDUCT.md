# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in or contributes to this project. We welcome individuals of all backgrounds, identities, skill levels, and perspectives.

## Expected Standards

Participants and contributors are expected to:
- Communicate with respect, kindness, and empathy.
- Provide constructive and actionable feedback.
- Respect differing points of view and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or offensive comments.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory remarks.
- Publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Any conduct that reasonably compromises the safety or dignity of others.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the project maintainer at **admin@knownaspratyush.online**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
