Featured Web Development Projects & Case Studies

Coffee & Cravings — Artisan Cafe & QR Table Ordering Platform

Role: Lead Full-Stack Web Developer & Systems Architect

Live Production URL: https://www.coffeeandcravings.shop/

Source Code: https://github.com/KNOWNASPRATYUSH/Coffee-cravings

Coffee & Cravings is a production-grade restaurant and cafe operating platform engineered with a zero-external-dependency native Node.js core on Vercel Serverless and Upstash Redis. Key capabilities include:

  • Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signed QR table ordering across 6 dining tables to eliminate tampering and fake orders.
  • Live Barista Kitchen Display System (KDS) featuring instant audio chimes and real-time order state lifecycles.
  • Dual payment rails supporting Razorpay webhook verification and dynamic UPI QR generation.
  • Full statutory compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023, automated data redaction, and cookie-free session tracking.
  • 100% automated test coverage with 71 passing integration test suites.
